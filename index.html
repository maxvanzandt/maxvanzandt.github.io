About

I am finishing an MA in Statistics and Data Science at UC Berkeley, where my coursework is built around causal inference, experimentation, and nonparametric methods. I want to work where the statistics is load bearing for the business rather than decorative, which in practice means product data science or biostatistics.

Before Berkeley I finished a BS in Statistics at UVA with a minor in philosophy, mostly analytic moral philosophy. The philosophy shows up more than you would expect: two of the projects below started as an argument I wanted to test by building it.

At Iridium I built an ML model predicting global flight routes that cut flight data pulls by 75%, and ran a survival analysis that surfaced subscriber provisioning lags of over 200 days, which fed into supply chain planning. At Biokind Analytics I lead statistical consulting for healthcare nonprofits, on questions like acute malnutrition relapse in children. I also led UVA's NFL Big Data Bowl entry through the Sports Analytics and Statistics Laboratory, and taught statistics as a TA.

Away from the desk I have played percussion for fifteen years. I lead a band that has opened for Neon Trees and releases music on Spotify, and I am an Eagle Scout.

Projects

Code and write ups live in their own repositories. The interactive pieces run live, so you can push on the analysis instead of reading my conclusions.

  1. 2026 Capstone, STAT 4996

    Album Covers and Billboard Success

    Predicts Billboard 200 success from cover art alone. Engineered a 70 dimensional visual feature space across 2,084 album covers using scikit-image and YOLO face detection, then fit L1 regularized logistic regression for 0.87 ROC-AUC. Texture, edge density, and the presence of a human face carried the most signal. Team project.

    • Python
    • scikit-learn
    • scikit-image
    • YOLO
    • APIs

    Repository

  2. 2025 Course project

    Drumming Styles: A Distributional Feature Analysis

    Asks whether individual drummers have a measurable statistical signature. Built a 32 dimensional feature space from 446,312 MIDI drum hits across 10 drummers, then applied Gaussian KDE, PCA capturing 63.8% of variance, and 3 state hidden Markov models to surface drummer specific persistence in playing state. Fifteen years behind a kit turned out to be useful for feature design.

    • Python
    • R
    • HMM
    • PCA
    • KDE

    Repository

  3. 2025 Course project

    Impact of Recession on Housing Starts Forecasting

    Compares time domain and frequency domain forecasting through the 2008 financial crisis. A SARIMA(1,0,1)(1,0,1)[12] fit showed regime dependent forecast failure, and spectral analysis located a shift in the underlying cycle from roughly 8 to 10.4 years. Co-authored with Sami Adam.

    • R
    • SARIMA
    • spectral analysis

    Repository

  4. 2025 Course project

    Kernel CCA for Market Analysis

    Applies kernel canonical correlation analysis to nine macroeconomic and market series from FRED, looking for nonlinear association that linear methods miss. A linear CCA baseline with a Wilks' Lambda test runs first, so the kernel extension has something to beat.

    • R
    • kernel methods
    • FRED

    Repository

  6. 2025 Interactive

    Publication Bias Simulation

    Turns an argument from the philosopher Liam Kofi Bright into a working model. If journals reward significant findings, what does the published record look like next to the research that was actually conducted? The publication model follows Moss and De Bin (2021). It illustrates a mechanism rather than estimating real publication rates.
